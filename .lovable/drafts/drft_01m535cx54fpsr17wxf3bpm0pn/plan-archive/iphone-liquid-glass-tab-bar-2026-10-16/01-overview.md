# iPhone "Liquid Glass" tab bar

Android keeps the look it has now. iPhones get a floating glass tab bar, like the one in your screenshot. This only changes phones.

## What changes on iPhone

- **Floating bar:** The tab bar no longer sits flush against the bottom edge. It becomes a rounded capsule with a gap on each side and a gap above the home indicator. Room tiles stay visible through it as they scroll underneath.
- **Glass material:** The bar is see-through, with a strong blur, a thin bright edge and a soft shadow, so it looks lifted off the page.
- **Selected tab:** The selected tab sits in its own darker glass capsule. Its icon and label use your chosen accent colour. Other tabs stay white or ink, depending on the theme.
- **Bolder icons:** Icons get filled shapes and a slightly heavier weight, as in the Apple Music example.
- **Top bar to match:** The slim top bar keeps its frosted see-through look, with a softer edge so it matches the floating bar.
- **Spacing:** The space kept at the bottom of the page grows a little, so the last row of rooms ends just above the floating bar.

Android, computers and tablets stay exactly as they are.
