# Phone layout tidy-up: slim top bar, no gap, progress bar back

These changes are for phones only. Computers and tablets stay as they are.

## 1. Slim top bar
- Remove the ☰ menu button from the top. The **More** tab already opens the same menu.
- Remove "The Hotel Hub" label, the hotel name and the "Shared workspace for…" text.
- In their place, put one narrow bar that runs edge to edge across the screen. It holds only the **Quick Setup** button, on the far right.
- Departments that don't use Quick Setup (F&B, Maintenance) get no top bar at all.

## 2. Remove the gap above the tab bar
The gap comes from three separate spacers stacked on top of each other. They will be replaced by one spacer sized to fit exactly what sits at the bottom:
- Housekeeping: the tab bar plus the progress bar.
- Other departments: the tab bar only.

After this, the last row of rooms ends just above the bars.
